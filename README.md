# 📊 Superstore Sales Dashboard (Power BI)

## 📌 Project Overview
This project is an interactive **Business Intelligence dashboard** built in **Microsoft Power BI** using the Superstore Orders dataset (51,000+ transactions).  

The dashboard provides clear insights into **sales performance, profitability, customer segments, regional trends, shipping efficiency, and product category performance**, helping stakeholders make data-driven business decisions.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives
The main goals of this Power BI project are:

- Track overall **Sales, Profit, and Quantity**
- Identify the best and worst performing **categories and sub-categories**
- Analyze **regional and market performance**
- Evaluate customer segment contribution to revenue and profit
- Monitor shipping mode performance and shipping cost impact
- Detect loss-making areas and business risks

---

## 📂 Dataset Description
The dataset contains transactional sales records including:

- Order details (Order ID, Order Date, Ship Date, Order Priority)
- Customer information (Customer ID, Segment)
- Geographic information (Country, Region, Market)
- Product details (Category, Sub-Category, Product Name)
- Business metrics (Sales, Profit, Discount, Quantity, Shipping Cost)

**Dataset Size**
- Rows: 51,305  
- Columns: 30+  

---

## 🛠 Tools Used
- **Microsoft Power BI Desktop**
- **Power Query** (data cleaning and transformation)
- **DAX** (calculated measures and KPIs)
- Interactive visualizations and slicers

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features
The Power BI dashboard includes:

### 🔹 KPIs
- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Quantity
- Average Discount
- Profit Margin

### 🔹 Sales & Profit Analysis
- Sales and profit trends over time
- Monthly and yearly performance comparison

### 🔹 Category & Product Performance
- Sales and profit by category and sub-category
- Top products by revenue and profitability

### 🔹 Regional / Market Performance
- Performance comparison by market and region
- Geographic breakdown of business performance

### 🔹 Customer Segment Analysis
- Sales and profit contribution by segment
- Identification of high-value customer groups

### 🔹 Shipping Analysis
- Ship mode distribution
- Impact of shipping cost on profitability

### 🔹 Discount & Loss Analysis
- Profitability impacted by discount levels
- Identification of loss-making products and categories

---

## 📌 Key Insights
This dashboard helps answer business questions such as:

- Which regions and markets generate the highest profit?
- Which product categories bring the most revenue but low profit?
- How do discounts affect profitability?
- Which customer segment contributes the most to profit?
- Which shipping mode is the most efficient?
